How your tower grows

  • One tower a day. Each day gets its own tower on your Morningside block. Tomorrow starts a new one.
  • 1 floor = {{ H.mpf }} minutes of deep focus. Deep focus is study you were really concentrating on. Rest and drift never remove floors.
  • Doors open at {{ H.door_min }} minutes (floor {{ H.door_floor }}). That is the day's floor: reach it and the day counts.
  • The crown lights at your daily target: {{ H.weekday_text }} on job days (floor {{ H.weekday_floor }}), {{ H.weekend_text }} on other days (floor {{ H.weekend_floor }}).
  • It tops out at {{ H.max_floors }} floors = {{ H.cap_text }}. Extra time is still shown, it just doesn't add floors or XP.
  • Each thing you ship adds a beacon on the roof.
  • Stars on the roof: one for your first log, one when the doors open, and one for {{ H.star_text }} of deep focus.
  • Late nights: your day runs until {{ H.day_starts }}, so 1:30 AM study counts for the day before. {% if H.cutoff_label %}Deep minutes after {{ H.cutoff_label }} are shown but don't count.{% else %}Every minute counts, no sleep cutoff.{% endif %}
