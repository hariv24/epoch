Tips for the tablet
  • On Android Chrome open the menu and choose Add to Home screen. Then open Epoch from that icon: it opens straight on the Focus Screen.
  • Tap the full-screen button on the Focus Screen to hide the browser bars.
  • To keep the screen on beside your laptop, set the tablet's screen timeout to the longest value (Settings, Display). While a run is going the page also asks the tablet to stay awake where Chrome allows it.
  • Sound needs one tap on the tablet after the page opens. Chimes follow the Sound switch above; soundscapes have their own picker.
